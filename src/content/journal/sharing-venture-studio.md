## Explaining a program beyond the classroom

As a founding partner and Director of Programs & Operations at Mason Venture Studio, I helped students connect their ideas with mentors and practical startup experience. Representing the program meant explaining more than my own work: it meant communicating what the student teams and the program were building together.

My source account describes presenting to visiting schools and educational leaders and to the Mason City Schools Board of Education. It also describes opportunities associated with the Ohio School Boards Association Capital Conference.

## What I wanted to communicate

The program gives students a place to identify a problem, test a business model, develop a product, and practice presenting an idea. During the initial program, we worked with 12 startup teams. Four advanced to competition and one qualified for national competition, according to the source.

The most valuable part of the work was often direct support: helping a student choose a next step or find a mentor with the right expertise.
