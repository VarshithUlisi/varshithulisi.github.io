## Explaining a program beyond the classroom

As a founding partner and Director of Programs & Operations at Mason Venture Studio, I helped students connect their ideas with mentors and practical startup experience. Representing the program meant explaining more than my own work: it meant communicating what the student teams and the program were building together.

I represented the program to visiting schools, educational leaders, and the Mason City Schools Board of Education, and presented at the Ohio School Boards Association Capital Conference.

## What I wanted to communicate

The program gives students a place to identify a problem, test a business model, develop a product, and practice presenting an idea. During the initial program, we worked with 12 startup teams. Four advanced to competition and one qualified for national competition.

The most valuable part of the work was often direct support: helping a student choose a next step or find a mentor with the right expertise.

## Taking the work to OSBA

On November 17, 2026, I presented Mason Venture Studio at the OSBA Capital Conference in Columbus. Our session reached around 40 superintendents and school board members and generated interest in visiting Mason’s student-led programs.
