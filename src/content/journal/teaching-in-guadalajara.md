## A weekly commitment

From September 2023 to June 2024, I volunteered as an English instructor with Aprendiendo Juntos in Guadalajara, Mexico. I taught for approximately two hours on Fridays, supporting Spanish-speaking community workers with language development and conversational English.

## Teaching for the learner

The learners came with different educational backgrounds. I adapted instruction to support those differences and worked to make the learning environment inclusive and focused on communication.

The experience was part of finding a way to contribute during my time in Mexico. Alongside Model United Nations, it gave me opportunities to practice speaking, listening, and communicating across perspectives.

## Carrying the experience forward

Later, my educational work in Ohio would involve younger students and different subjects. The context changed, but paying attention to the learner remained important.
